---
title: "ADR 0046: Failed deadline teardown receipts"
---

Status: Accepted for the `oka_core` command-scoped process seam.
Date: 2026-10-14
North Star impact: applies; failure completion within ADRs 0018 and 0026,
without changing signal authority or managed lease retention.

A real owned `sleep` reproduction with unavailable process-tree discovery
returned `TreeStopResult(stopped: false)` but `runBoundedProcess` still waited
for root exit. The caller never received the known failure. Killing without
discovery would violate ownership policy; waiting forever conceals the failure.

When deadline teardown completes unsuccessfully, finish the bounded-run result
even if root exit is not observed. Preserve the legacy deadline cause (a stop
request, not verified death), expose `rootExitObserved` and `rootIdentityToken`,
use exitCode -1 when no exit was observed, and include the unresolved root in
survivors. `teardownVerified: false` and failure detail remain mandatory.
Successful teardown still awaits root exit; root exit alone still awaits tree
checking.

Keep bounded output draining until an unresolved root exits. Canceling its
output subscriptions at refusal made a real writing fixture exit; closing a
live pipe is an unintended effect, not an authorized tree stop. The returned
capture remains bounded. Keeping this drainage may keep a standalone Dart
runner alive while recovery remains unresolved; no detached-runtime claim is
made.

The high-level runner preserves TimeoutException but reports unverified cleanup,
root identity and known survivors instead of claiming a kill or empty tree.
Callers needing recovery must retain the result/diagnostic and use existing
identity-gated cleanup or managed leases. This receipt grants no extra signal
authority and is not crash-safe managed recovery.

This bounds completion after a returned teardown failure. It does not establish
a hard bound for a platform probe that never completes, spawn/lease atomic
recovery, or discovery of descendants detached before the initial snapshot.
The regression owns and separately reaps its real fixture child; production
does not bypass the discovery refusal.

2026-10-14 follow-up: two real owned-child regressions also stalled initial
identity and subtree reads indefinitely. Add a configurable `observationBudget`
(default two seconds) shared by direct read-only observations in each tree stop.
Spend it only while awaiting reads, excluding grace waits and mutating adapter
calls. Budget exhaustion returns unverified survivors, never a delayed signal.
Startup identity capture uses the lesser of that budget and the remaining run
deadline; its elapsed time is subtracted before scheduling deadline teardown.
Without a captured startup identity, refuse deadline signals rather than
recapturing a possibly different root at stop time.

Timeout does not cancel an injected future or its resources. A late read can
complete but cannot resume the timed-out stop or confer authority. Mutating
`kill` adapters remain awaited: timing them out would allow effects after a
returned receipt. Platform spawn, kill-adapter completion and adapter-owned
probe subprocess cleanup do not acquire a hard completion guarantee here.
